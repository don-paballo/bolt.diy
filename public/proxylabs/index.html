Common Questions

Everything you need
to make a decision

How long does implementation take? +
Most clients have their first automation live within 4–6 weeks of kick-off. Full multi-system implementations (Momentum/Dominance) typically take 8–16 weeks depending on complexity. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery call before any commitment.
What does the performance fee mean exactly? +
The performance fee (15–20% depending on package) is calculated on the measurable cost savings and efficiency gains the automation delivers over the contract period. This means our income is directly tied to your results — if the automation doesn't perform, neither do we. Detailed metrics and calculation methodology are agreed upfront.
What software does your AI integrate with? +
We integrate with most major business tools: CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), ERPs (SAP, Sage), accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks), communication platforms (Slack, Teams, email), custom databases, and thousands of other tools via API. If your software has an API or webhook capability, we can likely integrate it. We'll confirm compatibility during discovery.
Does my team need technical knowledge to use the systems? +
No. We build monitoring dashboards and control interfaces that are designed for non-technical operators. Your team oversees the system, reviews outputs, and handles true exceptions — everything else runs automatically. We provide full onboarding and training as part of every package.
What if the automation makes a mistake? +
All systems include confidence scoring — when the AI is uncertain, it flags the task for human review instead of proceeding automatically. High-stakes actions have human-in-the-loop checkpoints built in. Our systems are also monitored continuously, and we respond to anomalies within hours.
Is my business data safe? +
Data security is built into every system we deliver. We implement role-based access controls, encrypted data transit and storage, and full audit trails. We operate under strict confidentiality agreements and can accommodate data residency requirements for South African businesses.
Why is the minimum investment R150,000? +
Custom AI automation — done properly — requires significant engineering time: discovery, architecture design, development, testing, integration, and training. Below a certain investment threshold, the ROI doesn't justify the complexity. The businesses that get the most from our work have meaningful manual workloads (40+ hours/week) where automation creates serious leverage.
